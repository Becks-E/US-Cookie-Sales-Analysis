# US Cookie Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes US cookie sales data to identify sales trends, customer purchasing patterns, top-performing products, and areas of business opportunity.

The analysis was carried out to transform raw sales data into meaningful insights that can support better business decisions.

## Objectives

The main objectives of this analysis were to:

* Analyze overall sales performance
* Identify the best-performing cookie products
* Compare sales across different regions
* Identify sales trends over time
* Understand customer purchasing patterns
* Generate actionable business insights

## Tools Used

* Microsoft Excel
* Power BI

## Analysis Process

The project involved:

1. Data cleaning and preparation
2. Data exploration
3. Data analysis
4. Data visualization
5. Dashboard development
6. Interpretation of business insights

## Key Insights

Some of the major findings from the analysis include:

* Identified the top-performing cookie products.
* Analyzed sales performance across different regions.
* Identified periods with higher and lower sales.
* Compared product performance to understand customer preferences.
* Highlighted areas where sales performance could be improved.

## Dashboard
The Power BI dashboard provides an interactive overview of US cookie sales performance, including product performance, sales trend, city-level performance, and customer purchasing patterns.

The dashboard was developed to transform the analyzed sales data into clear and actionable business insights.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the business could:

* Focus marketing efforts on high-performing products.
* Investigate the reasons behind low-performing products.
* Use sales trends to improve inventory planning.
* Develop targeted promotions based on customer purchasing patterns.
* Monitor regional performance to identify growth opportunities.
